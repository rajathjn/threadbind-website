## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a sampler stitched by the studio: headlines are cross-stitch, sections are whole cloths, and the hero is a loom you stitch a word into. It refuses the indie-studio default of dark key art, phone mockups and feature cards.
OWN-WORLD: Aida cloth fields at page scale (forest #0E3A25, oxblood #5C2230, saffron #F2B705, vermilion #E8452A) with the hole grid as texture; floss-cream thread #F6EBD3 for text on dark cloth; stitched 5x7 alphabet for display; Fraunces Soft and Nunito, the game's own faces, for set text; square-cornered saffron buttons; traced cells fill with solid stitches, trace is a dashed backstitch.
STORY: On home, visitors meet the studio (stitched THREADBIND, the satin-stitched lattice) and are sent to Weave. On Weave, they see WEAVE stitched, trace a word and watch bends raise the multiplier, understand a run is eight weaves, see the real game screens, and join the waitlist.
