Questions

When does Weave come out?

There is no release date yet. Weave is in development. Join the waitlist and we will email you when there is a build to play.

Which platforms will it be on?

iOS and Android. It is designed for phones held upright and played with one hand.

How much will it cost?

We have not announced pricing. We will say clearly before launch.

How does scoring work?

A word scores its base (the sum of its letter values) times its mult (its length plus one for every 90° bend in the trace). THREAD with two bends scores 11 × 8 = 88. You can try a loom on the Weave page.

What is a seed?

Every run comes from an eight-character code. Share it and a friend can play exactly the same run.

Can I stream or make videos of Weave?

Yes, and you can monetise them. Read the content policy for the details.

I played a test build. Where do I send feedback or a bug?

Use the feedback form. For bugs, include your device, its OS version and what you did just before it happened.

How do I get my data deleted?

Use the contact form or email threadbindstudios@proton.me and we will remove it. The privacy policy explains what we hold.
